<h3>3-1. bootc private registry 인증</h3><div class="code-card" data-lang="shell"><div class="code-head"><span>Shell · /etc/ostree/auth.json</span><div class="code-actions"><button aria-controls="explain-8" aria-expanded="false" class="explain-toggle" type="button">명령어 설명</button><button class="copy" type="button">복사</button></div></div><pre><code># sudo podman login으로 생성한 root용 Quay 인증을 bootc에도 제공
